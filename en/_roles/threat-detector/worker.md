The `threat-detector.worker` enables viewing logs registered in the customer's infrastructure using [{{ at-full-name }}](../../audit-trails/index.yaml).

The role is issued to the [service account](../../iam/concepts/users/service-accounts.md) to perform {{ td-full-name }}-driven security management and extends to an organization, cloud, or folder. This service account should be specified when [creating](../../security-deck/operations/workspaces/create.md) the workspace.
