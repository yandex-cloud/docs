The `ai.canvasAgents.viewer` role enables viewing text-based agents.

Users with this role can:


* View the list of [text-based agents]({{ link-docs-ai }}ai-studio/concepts/agents/text-agents) in the folder as well as info on them and their versions.
* View info on [access permissions](../../../iam/concepts/access-control/index.md) granted for text-based agents.
* View the list of asynchronous operations with text-based agents.
* View info on the relevant [folder](../../../resource-manager/concepts/resources-hierarchy.md#folder).


This role includes the `ai.canvasAgents.auditor` permissions.
