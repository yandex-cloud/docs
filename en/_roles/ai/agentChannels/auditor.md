The `ai.agentChannels.auditor` role enables viewing the metadata of channels for publishing text-based agents.

Users with this role can:


* View info on [channels for publishing text-based agents]({{ link-docs-ai }}ai-studio/concepts/agents/text-agents#publication-channels), as well as the list of channels in the folder.
* View info on [access permissions](../../../iam/concepts/access-control/index.md) granted for text-based agent publication channels.
* View info on the relevant [folder](../../../resource-manager/concepts/resources-hierarchy.md#folder).
