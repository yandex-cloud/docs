The `ai.agentChannels.admin` role enables managing channels for publishing text-based agents and access permissions granted for them.

Users with this role can:


* View info on [channels for publishing text-based agents]({{ link-docs-ai }}ai-studio/concepts/agents/text-agents#publication-channels), create, modify, and delete such channels, as well as suspend and resume their operation.
* View info on [access permissions](../../../iam/concepts/access-control/index.md) granted for text-based agent publication channels and modify such permissions.
* Publish text-based agent versions in channels.
* View info on the relevant [folder](../../../resource-manager/concepts/resources-hierarchy.md#folder).


This role includes the `ai.agentChannels.editor` permissions.
