The `ai.agentChannels.editor` role enables managing text agent publication channels and publish agent versions in them.

Users with this role can:


* View info on [channels for publishing text-based agents]({{ link-docs-ai }}ai-studio/concepts/agents/text-agents#publication-channels) and [access permissions](../../../iam/concepts/access-control/index.md) granted for such channels.
* Create, modify, and delete channels for publishing text-based agents, as well as suspend and resume their operation.
* Publish text-based agent versions in channels.
* View info on the relevant [folder](../../../resource-manager/concepts/resources-hierarchy.md#folder).


This role includes the `ai.agentChannels.viewer` permissions.
