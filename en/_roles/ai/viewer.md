

The `ai.viewer` role enables viewing info on quotas for [{{ translate-full-name }}]({{ link-docs-ai }}ai-studio/concepts/limits#yandexgpt-quotas), [{{ vision-full-name }}]({{ link-docs-ai }}ai-studio/concepts/limits#yandexgpt-quotas), [{{ speechkit-full-name }}]({{ link-docs-ai }}speechkit/concepts/limits#speechkit-quotas), and [{{ ai-studio-full-name }}]({{ link-docs-ai }}ai-studio/concepts/limits#yandexgpt-quotas), on [text generation models]({{ link-docs-ai }}ai-studio/concepts/generation/models), [guardrails]({{ link-docs-ai }}ai-studio/concepts/security/guardrails#rules) for model responses, [datasets]({{ link-docs-ai }}ai-studio/concepts/resources/dataset), uploaded [files]({{ link-docs-ai }}ai-studio/concepts/search/vectorstore#file-uploading), and {{ vector-store-name }} [search indexes]({{ link-docs-ai }}ai-studio/concepts/search/vectorstore) in {{ ai-studio-full-name }}, read such files and conduct searches using these indexes, as well as view info on the relevant [cloud](../../resource-manager/concepts/resources-hierarchy.md#cloud) and [folder](../../resource-manager/concepts/resources-hierarchy.md#folder).


This role includes the `ai.auditor`, `ai.assistants.viewer`, `ai.datasets.viewer`, `ai.models.viewer`, and `ai.guardrails.viewer` permissions.
