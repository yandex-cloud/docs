The `ai.subscriptions.editor` role enables users to manage their subscription.

Users with this role can:

* Get a list of [subscriptions]({{ link-docs-ai }}ai-studio/manage/concepts/subscription#subscription) in the [cloud](../../../resource-manager/concepts/resources-hierarchy.md#cloud).
* View info on their subscription.
* Purchase, use, modify, and delete their subscription.
* Reset consumption windows for any subscription in the cloud.

This role includes the `ai.subscriptions.user` permissions.
