The `ai.subscriptions.admin` role enables managing user subscriptions in the cloud.

Users with this role can:

* Get a list of [subscriptions]({{ link-docs-ai }}ai-studio/manage/concepts/subscription#subscription) in the [cloud](../../../resource-manager/concepts/resources-hierarchy.md#cloud) and view info on them.
* Purchase, modify, and delete subscriptions, including other users' subscriptions.
* Reset consumption windows for any subscription in the cloud.
* Use their subscription.

This role includes the `ai.subscriptions.editor` permissions.
