You can assign the `datalens.workbooks.editor` role to a workbook. With it, you can manage both the workbook and all its nested objects. In the {{ datalens-name }} UI, this role is referred to as `Editor`. You may want to only assign this role through the {{ datalens-name }} UI.

Users with this role can:


* Edit the relevant [workbook]({{ link-datalens-docs }}/workbooks-collections/) and create copies of it, as well as export and delete the workbook.
* View and edit all workbook's nested [objects]({{ link-datalens-docs }}/concepts/).
* View info on the [access permissions](../../../iam/concepts/access-control/index.md) granted for the workbook.


This role includes the `datalens.workbooks.viewer` permissions.
