You can assign the `datalens.workbooks.admin` role to a workbook. With it, you can manage the relevant workbook and access to it, as well as all its nested objects. In the {{ datalens-name }} UI, this role is referred to as `Admin`. You may want to only assign this role through the {{ datalens-name }} UI.

Users with this role can:


* View info on the [access permissions](../../../iam/concepts/access-control/index.md) granted for the relevant [workbook]({{ link-datalens-docs }}/workbooks-collections/) and modify such permissions.
* Edit, move, export, create copies of, and delete the relevant workbook.
* View and edit all workbook's nested [objects]({{ link-datalens-docs }}/concepts/).
* [Embed]({{ link-datalens-docs }}/security/private-embedded-objects) the workbook's nested private objects to websites and apps.
* [Publish]({{ link-datalens-docs }}/concepts/datalens-public#how-to-publish) the workbook's nested objects.


This role includes the `datalens.workbooks.editor` permissions.
