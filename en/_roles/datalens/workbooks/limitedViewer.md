

You can assign the `datalens.workbooks.limitedViewer` role to a [workbook]({{ link-datalens-docs }}/workbooks-collections/). With it, you can view all the workbook's nested [charts]({{ link-datalens-docs }}/concepts/chart/), [dashboards]({{ link-datalens-docs }}/concepts/dashboard), and [reports]({{ link-datalens-docs }}/reports/), as well as info on the [access permissions](../../../iam/concepts/access-control/index.md) granted for the workbook. In the {{ datalens-name }} UI, this role is referred to as `Limited viewer`. You may want to only assign this role through the {{ datalens-name }} UI.
