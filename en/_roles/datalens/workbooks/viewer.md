

You can assign the `datalens.workbooks.viewer` role to a [workbook]({{ link-datalens-docs }}/workbooks-collections/). With it, you can view all workbook's nested [objects]({{ link-datalens-docs }}/concepts/) and the info on the [access permissions](../../../iam/concepts/access-control/index.md) granted for such a workbook. In the {{ datalens-name }} UI, this role is referred to as `Viewer`. You may want to only assign this role through the {{ datalens-name }} UI.


This role includes the `datalens.workbooks.limitedViewer` permissions.
