To use the `billing.usagerecords.admin` role, you need to assign it for an [organization](../../../organization/concepts/organization.md), [cloud](../../../resource-manager/concepts/resources-hierarchy.md#cloud), or [folder](../../../resource-manager/concepts/resources-hierarchy.md#folder). It enables viewing resource consumption details without access to the [billing account](../../../billing/concepts/billing-account.md).
