The `cspm.viewer` role enables viewing info on cloud infrastructure checks for compliance with [security standards](../../security-deck/concepts/cspm.md#standards) and their results, as well as on jobs for such checks and [exceptions](../../security-deck/concepts/cspm.md#exceptions) from check rules configured in the [{{ cspm-name }}](../../security-deck/concepts/cspm.md) settings.

This role includes the `cspm.auditor` permissions.
