The `serverless.mcpGateways.viewer` role enables viewing info on [MCP servers]({{ link-docs-ai }}ai-studio/concepts/mcp-hub/#servers), [access permissions](../../../iam/concepts/access-control/roles.md) granted for them, and on [quotas]({{ link-docs-ai }}ai-studio/concepts/limits#yandexgpt-quotas) for using MCP servers.

This role includes the `serverless.mcpGateways.auditor` permissions.
