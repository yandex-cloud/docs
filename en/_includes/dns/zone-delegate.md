To delegate reverse zone management to {{ dns-name }}:

1. Confirm that you own the domain zone (dedicated IP). To do this, just provide a copy of the document confirming ownership right transfer and registration information from the [RIPE](https://www.ripe.net/) database that identifies IP ownership.
1. Send the document and RIPE information to the {{ yandex-cloud }} information security team. In your cover letter, describe the reason for your request and ask for a verification.

   The {{ yandex-cloud }} information security team will verify the data you provide and notify you of the results.
1. After your data has been successfully verified, [contact support](../../support/overview.md).
