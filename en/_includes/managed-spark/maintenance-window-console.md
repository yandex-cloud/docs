* To allow maintenance at any time, select **{{ ui-key.yacloud.mdb.forms.value_maintenance-type-anytime }}** (default).
* To specify a preferred maintenance start time, select **{{ ui-key.yacloud.mdb.forms.value_maintenance-type-weekly }}** and specify a day of the week and a UTC time interval. For example, you can choose the cluster's least busy time.

Both active and stopped clusters are subject to maintenance operations. These may include installing {{ SPRK }} updates and patches for hosts as well as other maintenance works.
