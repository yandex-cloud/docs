{% note info %}

When you create a metadata catalog, the **metadata AI markup** is on by default.

With this option enabled, the AI assistant suggests descriptions, [domains](*domain), [classifications](*classification) and [tags](*tag), [glossaries](*glossary) and [terms](*term), and marks up your metadata using them. You can confirm, edit, or reject any suggestion your AI assistant makes by hovering over the **{{ ui-key.yacloud.data-catalog.label_ai-label }}** icon next to the suggestion and selecting the action.

After the catalog is created, you can manage the AI markup on the **{{ ui-key.yacloud.common.overview }}** page or when [updating](../../metadata-hub/operations/data-catalog/update-catalog.md) the catalog.

{% endnote %}
