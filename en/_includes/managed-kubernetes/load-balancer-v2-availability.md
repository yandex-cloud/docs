{% note warning %}

The `v2` mode is available in the `RAPID` [release channel](../../managed-kubernetes/concepts/release-channels-and-updates.md) and must first be enabled on the {{ yandex-cloud }} side. To have it enabled, contact [support](../../support/overview.md) and provide the [cloud ID](../../resource-manager/operations/cloud/get-id.md) and [cluster ID](../../managed-kubernetes/operations/kubernetes-cluster/kubernetes-cluster-list.md#list) info.

Once it is enabled, configure the `v2` mode as detailed in [this guide](../../managed-kubernetes/operations/configure-load-balancer-target-groups.md).

{% endnote %}
