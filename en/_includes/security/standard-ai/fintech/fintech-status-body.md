The application is for reference only and introduces no regulatory requirements on its own. Actions listed under _User actions_ are recommended deficit elimination measures. They become mandatory only if approved by the organization's internal regulatory document. The compliance matrix does not replace the requirements of this Standard.

The application is used to design, develop, deploy, and operate AI solutions, including:

* Applications based on external and internal AI APIs.
* RAG systems, knowledge bases, vector stores, and data ingestion processes.
* AI agents, MCP servers, MCP Gateway, and connected tools.
* ML models, datasets, training, testing, fine-tuning, and deployment processes.
* Cloud infrastructure used for AI solutions.
