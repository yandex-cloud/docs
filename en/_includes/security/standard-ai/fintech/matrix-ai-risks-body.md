| External requirement | Status | Covered / lacking in the Standard | Relevant AI-* requirements | User actions | Responsibility |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Consider information security risks of AI when selecting a scenario, technology, architecture, and protection measures. | Partially | The Standard imposes technical measures for AI API, RAG, MCP, data, and cloud infrastructure, but the unified process of admissibility evaluation for scenarios and information security risks of the AI solution requires formalization. | `AI-SDLC1` | Before you start development, register the scenario in the AI solution registry; evaluate information security risks, process criticality, admissible automation level, and the need for human-in-the-loop; get the results approved by the business owner or information security officer. | User |
| Evaluate the consequences in terms of subject permissions, operations, costs, reputation, supply chain, and organization stability. | Not fulfilled | The Standard provides for confidentiality and compliance controls but has no formal procedure for evaluation of consequences for the specified categories. | `AI-SDLC1`, `AI-COMPLY1` | Perform and document the evaluation of consequences before commissioning the AI solution; determine unacceptable scenarios, limitations on automation, and conditions for escalation to a human. | User |
| Use human-in-the-loop for high-risk automatic operations. | Partially | The Standard provides for confirmation of individual actions via MCP Gateway but does not set the general rule for manual validation of AI results in critical processes. | `AI-AGENT-HITL1` | List critical operations; prohibit autonomous execution of such operations; provide the possibility to check, reject, and update the result by a human; log record the decision and responsible person. | User |
| Develop a threat model for the AI system. | Partially | The Standard describes the boundaries of Ingres, Identity, Application, Model, Data/RAG, Egress, and Logging/Monitoring contours; however, the duty to develop the threat model and keep it up-to-date must be formalized explicitly. | `AI-SDLC1` | Develop a threat model for each AI solution; include assets, interfaces, external and internal intruders, AI-specific threats, attack scenarios, and related controls; review the threat model in case of significant changes. | User |
| Make sure the measures are adequate to risk level and consequences. | Not fulfilled | The Standard lists protection measures, but there is no rule for how to select and justify measures depending on risk. | `AI-SDLC1` | Define a risk level for each AI solution; select the minimum set of required controls depending on risk level; document justification of exclusions and mitigation measures. | User |
