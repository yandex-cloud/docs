To assign roles for resources:

1. Select `{{ ui-key.yacloud_components.organization.access_tab_assign-roles }}`.
1. In the **{{ ui-key.yacloud_components.organization.access_label_scope }}** field, specify a resource to assign a role for.
1. Click ![plus](../../_assets/console-icons/plus.svg) **{{ ui-key.yacloud_components.organization.access_action_add-role }}** and select the required role from the list. To assign users multiple roles for the same resource, repeat this step.
1. To assign roles for another resource, click ![plus](../../_assets/console-icons/plus.svg) **{{ ui-key.yacloud_components.organization.access_action_add-scope }}** and repeat the previous steps.
