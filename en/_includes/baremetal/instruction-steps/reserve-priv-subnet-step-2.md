Under **{{ ui-key.yacloud.baremetal.title_common-info }}**:

1. In the **{{ ui-key.yacloud.baremetal.field_subnetwork-name }}** field, enter the name of the public subnet.
1. Optionally, in the **{{ ui-key.yacloud.baremetal.field_description }}** field, add a subnet description.
1. Optionally, under **{{ ui-key.yacloud.component.label-set.label_labels }}**, click **{{ ui-key.yacloud.component.label-set.button_add-label }}** and set [labels](../../../resource-manager/concepts/labels.md).
