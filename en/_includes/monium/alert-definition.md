An _alert_ is a check that determines a [status](../../monium/concepts/alerting/alert.md#alert-statuses) every minute based on predefined conditions. Each status has its own conditions, and the alert switches to that status when those conditions are met. Depending on the alert type, the conditions apply to [query](../../monium/concepts/querying.md#queries) results, [SLO](../../monium/concepts/alerting/alert.md#slo-alerts) metrics, or the statuses of [other alerts](../../monium/concepts/alerting/alert.md#composite-alerts).

Notifications about status changes are sent via the configured [channels](../../monium/concepts/alerting/notification-channel.md).
