#### Can I use my OS license for VM instances in {{ yandex-cloud }}? {#os-license}

Yes, you can bring your own licenses to {{ yandex-cloud }}, subject to your specific OS type and license terms.

Most Linux distributions use open-source licenses that do not restrict their use in cloud environments. You can [upload your own image](../../compute/operations/image-create/upload.md) and use it to create a VM.

For Microsoft products, the possibility to use your own license depends on your specific product terms and requires [dedicated hosts](../../compute/concepts/dedicated-host.md). For more information, see [{#T}](../../microsoft/byol.md).
