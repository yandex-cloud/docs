Maintenance can be postponed from its originally scheduled date:
* By a maximum of two and a half months for major {{ GP }} version updates, e.g., from 6.28.20 to 6.29.8.
* By a maximum of one month for updates between minor {{ GP }} versions, e.g., from 6.29.1 to 6.29.2.
* By a maximum of two weeks for all other types of maintenance.
