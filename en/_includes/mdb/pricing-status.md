## Cluster status {#running-stopped}

Pricing applies differently depending on the cluster status:

| **Status**                                    | **Pricing**                                                                  |
|-----------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| `Running`, `Creating`, `Stopping`, `Updating` | You pay for computing resources as well as storage and backup size. |
| `Stopped`, `Starting`                         | You only pay for storage and backup size.                          |
