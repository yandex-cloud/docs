In the **{{ ui-key.yacloud.marketplace-v2.title_request-access-to-product-dialog }}** window that opens:

1. Make sure the **{{ ui-key.yacloud.marketplace-v2.label_request-access-billing-account }}** field specifies the billing account linked to the clouds for which you want to open access to the product. Access will be granted for all the linked clouds.
1. Describe how you intend to use the product.
1. Click **{{ ui-key.yacloud.marketplace-v2.button_request-access-to-product }}**.
