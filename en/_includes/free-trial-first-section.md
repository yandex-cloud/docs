You can enable the trial period only when [creating your first billing account](../billing/quickstart/index.md) for a legal entity in {{ billing-name }}.

During the trial period, you can use {{ yandex-cloud }} resources for free with limitations on the trial duration and [initial grant](../billing/concepts/bonus-account.md#start) amount. You can view the info on applicable usage restrictions and grant amount in the [management console]({{ link-console-main }}) or agree them upon in supplemental agreements.

Resource usage costs are deducted from your initial grant. Yandex calculates service usage costs based on the pricing policy published on our website or in accordance with custom rates defined in your agreement. This way, you know exactly when your [trial period ends](../getting-started/free-trial/concepts/trial-ending.md) or your initial grant runs out.

During your free trial, Yandex may impose additional technical and functional limitations on service usage. Additionally, Yandex does not provide service availability guarantees outlined in the [Service Level Agreement](../overview/sla.md) (SLA).

You can check your remaining initial grant amount and trial days on the billing account page in [**{{ billing-name }}**]({{ link-console-billing }}). During the trial period, you can also [view statistics](../billing/operations/check-charges.md) on resource usage and charges based on the usage rate.

You can upgrade to the [paid version](../getting-started/free-trial/concepts/upgrade-to-paid.md) before the trial period ends. To do this, select the relevant settings in the [management console]({{ link-console-main }}). When you upgrade, the unused portion of your initial grant remains available and discounts your service costs. Also, the initial grant expires automatically once your trial period ends.
