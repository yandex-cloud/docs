To find out the connection ID:

1. In the [management console]({{ link-console-main }}), select a folder.
1. [Navigate]({{ link-console-main }}/link/metadata-hub) to **{{ ui-key.yacloud.iam.folder.dashboard.label_metadata-hub }}**.
1. Navigate to the **{{ ui-key.yacloud.iam.folder.dashboard.label_connection-manager }}** tab.
