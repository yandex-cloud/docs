# Getting commissions

To claim your rebate reward, request a payout in the Partner portal and submit the required documents.

{% note warning %}

You can only claim rewards if you have no arrears from previous reporting periods or other violations of the partner agreement terms.

{% endnote %}

## Step 1: Requesting a payout {#request}

{% list tabs group=instructions %}

- Partner portal {#partner}

  1. Go to the [partner portal]({{ link-cloud-partners }}).
  1. In the left-hand panel, select ![image](../../_assets/console-icons/scales-balanced.svg) **Finance**.
  1. In the left-hand panel, select ![icon](../../_assets/console-icons/sack.svg) **Rebate accrual balance**.
  1. In the **Available for withdrawal** window, click **Withdraw**.
  1. Specify the amount to withdraw.
  1. Select the withdrawal destination:

      * **Invoice deduction**: The amount will reduce the sum due for the consumption of your customer subaccounts in the current month.
      * **To bank account**: The amount will be transferred to the bank account specified in the partner agreement.

  1. Click **Withdraw**.

{% endlist %}

Your payout request is now submitted; wait to receive the document templates.


## Step 2: Submitting documents in the Russia region {#docs-ru}

Subject to your agreement terms, document templates will be emailed to you within seven business days after the [reporting period](../../billing/concepts/reporting-period.md) ends.

* Partners with enabled [electronic document management](../../billing/operations/edo.md) receive document templates directly in their EDM system account. Sign the documents directly from the request sent by {{ yandex-cloud }}. Do not create a new request.

* Non-EDM users receive document templates by email, along with submission instructions.

## Step 3: Receiving payment in the Russia region {#get-payment-ru}

1. VAT payers receive payout based on submitted documents within 15 business days after the reporting period ends. Partners who fail to submit their documents by the 25th day of the following month will receive this payment together with the next reporting period's payout.

1. Non-VAT payers receive payment within 15 business days after the end of the reporting period, regardless of whether the originals were submitted.
