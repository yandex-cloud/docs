# Viewing activity info

Under ![icon](../../_assets/console-icons/list-timeline.svg) **Activities**, you can find information about partners' tools used as part of a deal (whether it is a deal, a scheduled procurement deal, or a project).

You can also open the **Activities** section from a specific deal, specifically from its info page.

For activities, you can view created applications.
