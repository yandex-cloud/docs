
Before getting started, register in {{ yandex-cloud }}, set up a [community]({{ link-docs }}/datasphere/concepts/community), and link your [billing account]({{ link-docs }}/billing/concepts/billing-account) to it.
1. [On the {{ ml-platform-name }} home page]({{ link-datasphere-main }}), click **Try for free** and select an account to log in with: Yandex ID or your working account with the identity federation (SSO).
1. Select the [{{ org-full-name }} organization]({{ link-docs }}/organization/) you are going to use in {{ yandex-cloud }}.
1. [Create a community]({{ link-docs }}/datasphere/operations/community/create).
1. [Link your billing account]({{ link-docs }}/datasphere/operations/community/link-ba) to the {{ ml-platform-name }} community you are going to work in. Make sure you have a linked billing account and its [status]({{ link-docs }}/billing/concepts/billing-account-statuses) is `ACTIVE` or `TRIAL_ACTIVE`. If you do not have a billing account yet, create one in the {{ ml-platform-name }} interface.
