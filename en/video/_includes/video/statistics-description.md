The following data is available:

* Number of views: Total number of views, integer.
* Number of viewers: Number of unique viewers, integer.
* Total viewing time: Total viewing time in hours and minutes.
* Average viewing time: Average viewing time in hours and minutes.
* Linear chart with total views and users plotted per unit of time.

To analyze your audience, you can use:

* Pie chart with distribution of views by device type.
* Pie chart with distribution of views by OS.
