1. In the [management console]({{ link-console-main }}), select a folder.
1. [Navigate]({{ link-console-main }}/link/storage) to **{{ ui-key.yacloud.iam.folder.dashboard.label_storage }}**.
1. Select the bucket from the list.
1. Select the object, then click ![link](../../_assets/console-icons/link.svg) **{{ ui-key.yacloud.storage.file.button_generate }}** on the top panel to generate a download link.
1. For a bucket with restricted access, specify the link **{{ ui-key.yacloud.storage.file.label_lifetime }}** in hours or days (the maximum time is 30 days) and click **{{ ui-key.yacloud.storage.file.button_generate }}**.
1. Copy the link.
